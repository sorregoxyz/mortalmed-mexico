# 05. The pharmakon multiple

*2026-10-17. Reconstructed on 2026-10-20 from working notes and conversations.*

## What I did
Joined the grant's term and mine: the **pharmakon multiple**. Pharmakon names the doubleness of remedy and poison; multiplicity offers a way to study it ethnographically.

I reduced the five lives to four enactments that can be followed in the field:
- **Licensed death**: killing made legitimate through professional authority. In Mexico, veterinary euthanasia.
- **Legible death**: the drug made knowable through classification and documents.
- **Desired death**: the drug as an object of hope, fear and planning, often without anything moving.
- **Material death**: dose, formulation, failure.

**Contested** stopped being a fifth life and became the thread running through all four: who decides which version counts.

## What I noticed
The most revealing moments are points of friction, where versions have to be coordinated and fail to be: a counter where a veterinary product meets a non-veterinary request, a seizure, a supply restriction, a clinic deciding who qualifies.

## Open questions
- How does this connect to inequality? Through access: who can reach a "good death", and who cannot.
