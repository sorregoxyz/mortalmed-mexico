# 13. Choosing where to stand

*2026-10-20. Reconstructed on 2026-10-20 from working notes and conversations.*

## What I did
Added a section for possible field sites, read from a third file, `data/sites.csv`.

## The reasoning
- **Mexico City**: where the drug is classified. COFEPRIS and SENASICA, the first Ley de Voluntad Anticipada (2008), INCan, the densest veterinary network, UNAM and CIESAS.
- **Guadalajara**: a contrasting legal and moral setting. No Ley de Voluntad Anticipada, a 2009 constitutional reform protecting life from conception, organised Catholic opposition.
- **The third site, to be decided with the team.** Tijuana is on the border, already in the documentary record, and where both customs regimes meet. Monterrey has a stronger institutional base and no "Mexico option" baggage, but it is not on the border.

## Decision
Two main sites and a third to be decided with the team, showing both options. The label on each card now reads "Ley de Voluntad Anticipada", the name people use, instead of my English translation.
