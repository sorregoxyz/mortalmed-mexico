# 04. An atlas, and the question of the book

*2026-10-16. Reconstructed on 2026-10-20 from working notes and conversations.*

## What I did
Imagined an Ontological Atlas of Pentobarbital: a research instrument recording not only where the drug appears but what it becomes in each encounter. Six dimensions per entry: enactment, actors, materials, permissions, connections, frictions.

## What I noticed
The atlas makes multimodal work part of the analysis, not a way of presenting results. It would show what network maps hide: incompatible classifications, overlapping jurisdictions, relations that exist through documents or expectations rather than physical transfer.

Then the question of scale. A book and an atlas sounds ambitious for a subproject. In an ERC team the monograph usually belongs to the PI, and four years with long fieldwork leaves little time to write one well.

## Decisions
- Articles first: one theoretical, two ethnographic, contributions to the comparative work.
- The atlas as the subproject's instrument. A book only later, if it grows naturally and in agreement with the PI.

## Open questions
- What stays in a restricted archive and what becomes public? Decide this before collecting anything.
