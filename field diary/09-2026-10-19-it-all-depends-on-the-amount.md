# 09. It all depends on the amount

*2026-10-19. Reconstructed on 2026-10-20 from working notes and conversations.*

## What I did
Reread two of my own texts against the project: the Samper Mendoza herb-market article and *Toxic Afterlives*. Kept the first as a source on the map.

## What I noticed
"Todo depende de la cantidad" is a vendor's version of the pharmakon. The herb market already contains the scenes the Mexico subproject needs:
- the stall as a situated clinic, where a seller reads a request and decides;
- a printed label and a spoken correction as two regimes of evidence on one product;
- an ethics of composite scenes and of not reproducing risky instructions.

From *Toxic Afterlives* one tension: I argued that specimenography is not controversy mapping, that specimens do not add up. Yet the map is a controversy map.

## Decisions
- The map is a controversy cartography, a tool for orientation. The atlas will be specimenographic: each entry held on its own terms.
- Bring the arguments of the herb market into the project, not the word "fragmentography". Pentobarbital is not a fragment.
- Remove the journal's name from the map while the article is under review.
