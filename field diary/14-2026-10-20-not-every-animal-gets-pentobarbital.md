# 14. Not every animal gets pentobarbital

*2026-10-20. Reconstructed on 2026-10-20 from working notes and conversations.*

## What I did
Corrected a mistake. I had justified Guadalajara through pig farming. But pentobarbital is not used routinely in the swine industry: it is expensive, controlled, and leaves the carcass toxic and unusable for rendering or meat. On farms it is used where the animal is valuable and already outside the food chain: large dairy operations, elite cattle, stud farms.

## What changed
Guadalajara holds up, for better reasons: Jalisco leads national milk production, it is the main centre of charrería and its horses, and the metropolitan area has a dense companion-animal market.

## What I noticed
The correction is an argument. The method of killing tracks the value of the animal. Who gets the expensive, controlled death says how a life is valued: dairy cows, horses and pets do; pigs do not. "Licensed death" has an economy.

And the carcass becomes a question. A body that cannot be rendered has to go somewhere. The drug persists in it. This is where my work on toxic afterlives meets the project without being forced.
