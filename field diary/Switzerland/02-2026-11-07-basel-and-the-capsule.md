# 02. Basel, and the capsule

*2026-11-07. Written the same day, from desk research.*

## What I did
Looked at Basel, the subproject's field site, through its most visible organisation, Pegasos, and then at the one attempt to do without pentobarbital altogether: the Sarco capsule in Schaffhausen. Press coverage from Swissinfo, Blue News, ITV and secondary reporting on The Times.

## What I found
- **Pegasos.** Founded in Basel in 2019. Unlike other organisations, it requires medical expert reports only in medically complex cases.
- **Price.** EXIT Suisse Romande charges CHF 100; Pegasos around CHF 10,000. A Basel lawyer quoted by Swissinfo ties this to Article 115: if someone gets rich, it is no longer altruistic.
- **Controversy, mostly about foreigners and families.** Repeated cases of people whose relatives were not told, including a Welsh woman, Anne Canning, in January 2025, after Pegasos had promised to always inform families. The Times estimated around 65 British people a year are accepted there.
- **Sarco.** In September 2024, a capsule that replaces oxygen with nitrogen was used for the first time, in a forest near Merishausen (Schaffhausen). Its organisers presented it as a drug-free death. Several people were arrested; the health minister said it was not legally compliant. The co-president of the organisation behind it was held for over two months and released once prosecutors ruled out intentional homicide; the investigation for inciting and aiding suicide continued. No final outcome found.

## What I noticed
The Sarco is the clearest proof of entry 01. It was designed to remove the prescription, and removing the prescription meant removing pentobarbital and the doctor in a single move. The state's answer was arrests. Pentobarbital is not only the means in Switzerland; it is what makes the act legible as legal. A death without it becomes a police matter.

Pegasos shows the other edge. The law asks only for non-selfish motives, so the price of a death becomes a legal question, not just an ethical one. The drug may cost little; what costs is the organisation around it.

For fieldwork: if Basel is the site, Pegasos is the most visible and contested actor in it. Access and positioning will need care, and the drug may be a better entry point than the controversy. Following the prescription, the pharmacy and the glass rather than the scandal.

## Open questions
- What other organisations operate in and around Basel, and how do they differ in their relation to the drug (who prescribes, who fetches it, who keeps it)?
- How do Basel's cantonal authorities and prosecutors handle each death? Every assisted suicide is followed by a medico-legal investigation.
- Is nitrogen a real alternative or a provocation? What would assisted dying without pentobarbital look like in Swiss law?
- How do foreign visitors move through the chain: who prescribes for someone who arrived last week?
