# 15. Reading the post against the map

*2026-10-20. Reconstructed on 2026-10-20 from working notes and conversations.*

## What I did
Read the job description line by line against the map. It asks about "the formal and informal networks through which sodium pentobarbital is accessed, sold, regulated, and transported", Mexico's role in transnational circulation, and people who "encounter, handle, sell, prescribe, discuss, or seek access to the drug."

## Gaps found and filled
- Transport: ANAM (Mexican customs), US Customs and Border Protection, couriers and postal channels.
- Prescribing: the special prescription regime for group II psychotropics (to verify).
- Informal networks: informal resale, intermediaries, online marketplaces and forums, described only as categories, by how they work and are policed.
- People seeking access, with their own ethics protocol.
- Methods named in the post: Shapiro & Kirksey on chemo-ethnography (2017), Ong & Collier on assemblages (2005).

## A shift
I had wanted to keep the border light. The post puts it at the centre. What to avoid is the "suicide tourism" register, not the border. Tijuana is now the strongest candidate for the third site.

## The PI's work
Added three of his texts: "States of Uncertainty" (2019), "Protecting Life, Facilitating Death" (2020), "Afterlife Reverberations" (2022). The real overlap is uncertainty: people living with plural, unclear rules, as in the swine fever outbreak and as in Mexico now. Afterlives meet too, in different senses: his are the social afterlives of the dead, mine the material afterlives of substances. Pentobarbital has both.
