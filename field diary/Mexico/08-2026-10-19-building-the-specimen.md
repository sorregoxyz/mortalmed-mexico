# 08. Building the Specimen

*2026-10-19. Reconstructed on 2026-10-20 from working notes and conversations.*

## What I did
Rebuilt the map. Lines now carry a type: institutional (solid), informational (dashed), material (dotted), scholarly (faint). An enactment filter follows the four entry points. Every entry has a status, checked or *to verify*, shown as a dashed ring. A register lists every entry with its source.

Added animals: Morris, *Blue Juice* (2012), named after pentobarbital solution itself; Sanders, "Killing with Kindness" (1995); Haraway, *When Species Meet* (2008); my own chapter on rats in Medellín.

## What checking turned up
- The 2024 US case is no longer only an allegation: a guilty plea followed in February 2025. The prosecutors' release says the drug is "sold in Mexico for the purpose of euthanizing animals."
- Pentobarbital is listed in group II of article 245 of the Ley General de Salud: some therapeutic value, a serious public health problem. A registered veterinary product and a scheduled psychotropic at once.
- Hayden's book is *The Spectacular Generic* (2023), not "Spectral". I had the title wrong from memory.

## Lesson
A map with verification flags is more honest than a map without them, and more useful: it is also a to-do list.
