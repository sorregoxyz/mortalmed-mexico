# 01. Reading the grant

*2026-10-07. Reconstructed on 2026-10-20 from working notes and conversations.*

## What I did
The interview invitation arrived: a panel, about twenty minutes, by video. Before thinking about answers I went to the grant itself. The CORDIS record gives the basics: an ERC Starting Grant at the University of Glasgow, May 2026 to April 2031, three sites (Swiss assisted dying, US executions, Mexican pharmaceutical markets).

## What I noticed
Two words carry the project. **Necro-sociality**: spaces where death is managed, governed and produced. **Pharmakon**: sodium pentobarbital as remedy and poison at once. The grant also frames the drug's circulation as tied to socio-economic and racial inequalities in the politics of death. Mexico is described through over-the-counter markets, which is a more precise starting point than the border stories I knew from the press.

## Decisions
- Use the PI's own vocabulary in the interview, not only mine.
- Begin with regulation and structure (COFEPRIS, state laws, veterinary distribution), with the border as one documented dimension.

## Open questions
- What does "over the counter" mean in practice for a scheduled substance?
- Where does Mexico sit in the comparison: supplier, loophole, or something else?
