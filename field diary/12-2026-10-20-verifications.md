## What I clarified
The Ley General de Salud and the Ley de Voluntad Anticipada are not the same law.
- The federal **Ley General de Salud**, in its palliative-care title (Título Octavo Bis), obliges doctors to respect a terminally ill patient's decisions about treatment (166 Bis 15), forbids extraordinary measures without consent (166 Bis 17), and prohibits euthanasia, "entendida como homicidio por piedad", and assisted suicide (166 Bis 21).
- The state **Leyes de Voluntad Anticipada**, starting with Mexico City in 2008, let a person refuse life-prolonging treatment in advance. As of July 2024, fifteen states had one; Jalisco and Baja California did not.

The federal law draws the line: refusing treatment is allowed, causing death is not. The state laws give people a way to refuse in advance.
