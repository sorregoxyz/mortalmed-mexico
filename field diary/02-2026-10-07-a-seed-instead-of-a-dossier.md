# 02. A seed instead of a dossier

*2026-10-07. Reconstructed on 2026-10-20 from working notes and conversations.*

## What I did
I considered sending the panel a research dossier and decided against it. Instead I started a small network map: fifteen entries (agencies, laws, the INCan ethnography, veterinary markets, the AVMA, advocacy discourse, the Church, the press), linked by simple lines and coloured by register.

## What I noticed
The map is the same move as *Porous Containment*: a documentary cartography of a controversy, applied to a drug instead of a virus. Building it forces precision. Every entry needs a source, and every line needs a reason.

## Decisions
- Keep the map at the institutional, legal, academic and discursive register. No vendors, no routes, no methods of acquisition. This publication filter is part of the method, not a limitation.
- Offer it if asked; never send it unsolicited.
- Frame it on the page as a working draft.

## Open questions
- What kind of line is a "connection"? A reference, a flow, a dispute?
