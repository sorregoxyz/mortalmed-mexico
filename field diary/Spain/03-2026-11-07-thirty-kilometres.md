# 03. Thirty kilometres

*2026-11-07. Written the same day, from desk research.*

## What I did
Before turning the first two entries into data, I asked how much weight Barcelona actually carries. Is the drug made here? Followed it backwards: company directories for the raw ingredient, the EU veterinary medicines database for the vials, the Catalan health service's dispensing order and protocol for the human solution, and the 2024 Catalan report for the deaths themselves.

## What I found
- **The molecule, possibly.** Moehs Ibérica, in Rubí, is the headquarters of the MOEHS group, which has produced active pharmaceutical ingredients since 1962, with four plants in Spain and others in Belgium, Germany (Chemische Fabrik Berg) and France. Industry directories list Moehs Ibérica and Chemische Fabrik Berg among suppliers of pentobarbital sodium. A listing does not say where the molecule is synthesised. Unverified; do not put it on a public map yet.
- **The veterinary vial, yes.** Eutanax has been authorised in Spain since 1992. Fatro Ibérica (Sant Just Desvern) holds the authorisation; Labiana Life Sciences (Terrassa, founded 1958, plants in Terrassa and Barberà del Vallès) releases the batches.
- **And it travels.** Labiana is also the batch release site for Allfatal vet., a euthanasia product authorised in Sweden. Pentobarbital leaves the Vallès for Scandinavia.
- **One hospital for every oral dose.** Catalonia's dispensing order for assisted dying says that, for oral self-administration, the dispensing pharmacy is always Hospital Universitari Vall d'Hebron. Every oral assisted death in Catalonia passes through one hospital pharmacy in Barcelona. Preparation, storage, dispensing and administration follow a Catalan pharmacological protocol from the Servei Català de la Salut.
- **The deaths.** 142 assisted deaths in Catalonia in 2024, 109 of them in the Barcelona demarcation. Hospital and home were level, at 39.5% each.
- **Aside for Switzerland.** The same directories list Siegfried, founded in Zofingen, among pentobarbital sodium suppliers. Worth following for the Swiss post.

## What I noticed
I had Barcelona down as convenient: where I live, where I have access. It is more than that. Within about thirty kilometres sit a possible producer of the molecule (Rubí), a plant that releases veterinary vials and exports them (Terrassa), the company that holds their authorisation (Sant Just Desvern), and the single pharmacy that compounds every oral dose for humans in Catalonia (Vall d'Hebron). From molecule, to vial, to glass.

That is a route, and a route can be walked. The Vallès Occidental as a corridor of pentobarbital: an industrial landscape I drive through without seeing what it makes. The map could be drawn as an itinerary, not a network.

Centralisation also matters for the argument. Cantabria routes its compounding through Valdecilla (entry 01); Catalonia routes it through Vall d'Hebron. The handmade human drug is not dispersed. It is concentrated in a few hospital rooms, which makes it both rare and locatable.

The two-site design from entry 02 holds, but tilts: Barcelona as main site, where the drug's lives are concentrated; Navarra or Asturias as the place where its afterlife in carcasses becomes visible.

## Open questions
- Where is the pentobarbital that Vall d'Hebron compounds sourced? The same industrial chain as the vials, or imported raw material?
- Is pentobarbital sodium actually synthesised in Rubí, or only sold from there?
- How much of what Labiana releases stays in Spain, and how much is exported?
- Access: hospital pharmacists seem reachable (entry 01); companies probably much less. What would a corporate ethnography of a contract manufacturer look like here?
