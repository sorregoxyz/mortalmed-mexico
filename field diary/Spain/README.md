# Field diary: a possible Spain fieldsite

Entries tracing a first desk exploration of sodium pentobarbital in Spain, opened after the PI mentioned that MORTALMED might add a Spanish fieldsite if the ERC approves it.

**Context.** After the Mexico post went elsewhere, I shared the Building the Specimen map with the PI. He replied that he was considering a Spanish fieldsite around compounded pentobarbital, environmental issues, the more-than-human, and assisted dying, with no promises, and suggested I apply to the open Research Associate post (Switzerland subproject, *Lethal Prescriptions*) to stay in the pool. I applied on 2026-11-04. These entries follow his four directions.

**Note on method.** Desk research only, carried out on 2026-11-05 and written up on 2026-11-07. Nothing here has been checked in the field.

### This is a thinking device, not a proposal. Whether a Spanish fieldsite exists, and what shape it takes, is the PI's decision and depends on the ERC. Switzerland belongs to the post I applied for; the project's wider mapping of pentobarbital's circulation belongs to the PharmaFlows PhD. This map is a tool for preparing fieldwork, not the project's map.
