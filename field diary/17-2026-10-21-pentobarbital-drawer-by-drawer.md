# 17. Pentobarbital, drawer by drawer

This is a tentative and initial working proposal that can be adjusted as much as the project requires that. It has been imagined as a four-year research plan. Each year puts one life of the drug in focus and builds the atlas entries for it. That life is usually written up the following year, once the material has settled. Contested life is not a year of its own. It runs through all four as the same question in a new setting: who decides which version counts here?

---

### Year one - Regulatory life
## The drug on paper
> How does pentobarbital exist in documents, and where do those documents disagree?

#### Rationale
Start where the drug is most stable and most accessible. Ethics review and onboarding make Year 1 desk-heavy anyway, so the year works with that constraint rather than against it. The regulatory life also sets the classifications that every other life will be measured against. Knowing who regulates whom tells you whom to ask in the field, so this year doubles as preparation for access.

#### Methods
- Documentary cartography of controversies. The Porous Containment method applied to a new object. Corpus: General Health Law, the Group II psychotropic schedule, NOM-033-SAG/ZOO-2014, COFEPRIS product registrations, state Voluntad Anticipada laws, relevant court rulings, US indictments.
- Classification tracing. Following Bowker and Star, map where a single product sits in several schedules at once, and which body holds each one.
- Discourse archive. Press, right-to-die advocacy material and veterinary trade publications, read as enactments of the drug, never as evidence that a transaction happened.
- Access map. Veterinary colleges and associations, distributors, regulators and palliative care units, ranked for the scoping visit.

#### Atlas in Practice
A0 fixes the entry template and the two-layer filter. A1 fills the documentary layer, completing the permissions dimension first, since that is what documents do best. Building the Specimen nodes migrate in as the first entries.

#### Ethics and Limits
Ethics approval, data management plan, and the written rule for what never becomes public. The autumn scoping visit tests access through exploratory conversations and does not count as data collection.

#### Output
P1 The Pharmakon Multiple &nbsp;
*Theoretical, desk-based. Pentobarbital as one substance enacted as medicine, regulated object, means of death and object of hope, and who decides which version counts. Built from literature, regulation and the existing cartography.* &nbsp;
Targets: Medical Anthropology Quarterly; Social Studies of Science.

### Year two - Veterinary Life
## The drug in the clinic
> How is killing made routine, and what makes it a good death?

#### Rationale
Veterinary euthanasia is where killing with pentobarbital is ordinary, licensed and legitimate. That makes it the hinge of the subproject: the desired and legible lives only make sense against this routine. It also needs the longest fieldwork, because practice is visible only in situ. By now ethics is approved and access has been prepared.

#### Methods
- Participant observation. Small-animal clinics in CDMX; dairy, equine and charrería settings and companion-animal practice in Guadalajara; veterinary pharmacies and distributors (ordering, storage, controlled-drug logbooks).
- Semi-structured interviews. Vets, technicians, pharmacy staff, distributors and veterinary school faculty. Owners after euthanasia only with care and a light touch.
- Follow the vial. An object biography of one registered product from registration to disposal, including the carcass and its afterlife (residues make it unusable for rendering).
- Multispecies attention and fragment collection. Animal bodies, labels, logbook pages and packaging, photographed with permission, gathered into a field manual and diary.

#### Atlas in Practice
A2: one entry per encounter, written within 48 hours while it is fresh. Interviews and identifying detail stay in the restricted archive. The public layer takes only institutional and practice-level material. Open the template to the other sites if the PI wants it.

#### Ethics and Limits
Informed consent, anonymity for practitioners, and attention to owners' grief. Nothing records how the drug might be diverted from the clinic.

#### Output
P2 An Atlas for an Unstable Object &nbsp;
*Method paper on the Ontological Atlas: the six dimensions, the two-layer publication filter, and what an atlas shows that a network map hides. Written once v1.0 has field entries.* &nbsp;
*Targets:* Visual Anthropology Review; Multimodality & Society (short version in Multimodal Studio)
