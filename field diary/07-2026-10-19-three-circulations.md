# 07. Three circulations

*2026-10-19. Reconstructed on 2026-10-20 from working notes and conversations.*

## What I did
Expanded the state of the art and checked it against what I had assumed.

## What I noticed
- **Three circulations**, not one: material (the product moves), informational (knowledge, claims and stories move), institutional (categories, standards and enforcement move). They intersect but must not be conflated. An online claim does not prove that a product travelled. An indictment documents particular conduct, not the size of a market.
- **The law is not one thing.** Advance directives, refusal of treatment, palliative sedation, euthanasia and assisted dying are different practices with different legal instruments and jurisdictions.
- **Two regulators.** COFEPRIS for human medicines and controlled substances, SENASICA for veterinary products. How they divide a veterinary product that contains a scheduled substance is a research question in itself.

## Corrections
- The "14 states" figure came from a 2019 count. Recount before citing.
- "Life from conception" provisions cannot be described as a categorical block without state-by-state legal analysis.

## Gap
No animals. The veterinary life is the hinge of the subproject, and the literature had none of it.
