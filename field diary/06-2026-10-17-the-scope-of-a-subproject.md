# 06. The scope of a subproject

*2026-10-17. Reconstructed on 2026-10-20 from working notes and conversations.*

## What I did
Reread yesterday's framework and narrowed it. Everything I had written for three sites now speaks only through Mexico.

## What I noticed
A framework can be generous to a project and still overstep it. The comparison belongs to the project as a whole. A subproject's contribution is to make its own site speak clearly enough that the comparison becomes possible.

## Decisions
- Describe the four enactments only through Mexican material.
- Present the atlas as how I would organise the Mexico fieldwork, with one sentence saying the format could be useful to others if it fits the team's plans.
- Name what Mexico brings: it is where "good death" is most routine, because the deaths are animal deaths; it shows circulation through stories and documents; it makes inequality concrete.
- Keep questions about the comparison for the end, as questions.
